# Third-party assets

## OpenMoji icons

`public/images/product-icons/` contains a curated subset of the [OpenMoji](https://openmoji.org/) icon set,
used as predefined images for products and categories.

- Source: https://github.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji
- License: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/#)
- Files are named by their Unicode hexcode (e.g. `1F969.svg`), unmodified from upstream.

Per the license, any redistribution of these files (modified or not) must carry the same attribution
and license terms.
